FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need to share my name?

No. The form does not ask for your name or contact details.

Will every question be answered?

We may combine similar questions, and not every submission can be included.

Where can I watch the answers?

Selected questions will be answered in videos shared through our church's YouTube channel and social media.

Can I submit more than one question?

Yes. Please submit one question at a time.

Is this the place for a private prayer request?

For private prayer support, please use our Prayer Request section.

Your privacy matters

  • This form does not request your name, email or contact details.
  • Authorized church team members can review submissions.
  • Selected questions may be edited for clarity and discussed publicly in recorded videos.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts run on this page.

Hosting note: The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may collect standard server logs (IP address, access time) as part of normal web hosting. These logs are managed by the provider and are separate from question data. We recommend the church reviews the GitHub Pages data collection policy and configures a data retention period that meets the church's needs.
