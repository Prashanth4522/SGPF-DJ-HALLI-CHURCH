Our Story

About Our Church

Shekinah Gospel Prayer Fellowship, Deverajeevanahalli Branch was born on September 27, 1992. We are a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled church dedicated to serving God and our community.

Our History & Calling

Shekinah Gospel Prayer Fellowship, Deverajeevanahalli Branch is a body of Christ with a divinely called ministry. Shekinah, D.J.Halli Branch was born on September 27, 1992 as per the leading of Lord Jesus Christ. It is a bible believing, Spirit filled Church with fruits and gifts operating.

We together as congregation, seek to serve God and others in our community, the commonwealth and around the world in His name. We welcome you to come and worship with us, experience Christian Fellowship and God's word from Pastor Joyson Dayalan at Shekinah Church, D J Halli Branch, Bangalore-45.

Our Vision

To propagate the gospel of Lord Jesus Christ to India and the world to know and to worship God as revealed in Lord Jesus Christ. To promote continued spiritual growth among the members.

Our Goal

To bring the Gospel Light to the city of Bangalore where darkness still prevails.

Our Community

Our Pastor

About Our Pastor

Joyson Sridhar Dayalan was dedicated to the work of the Lord from childhood. After completing his B.Com degree, he committed himself to full-time ministry in 2008 following a season of deep spiritual transformation, during which God stirred his heart, broke old bonds, and birthed within him a strong desire for the work of the Kingdom.

In 2008, Pastor Joyson moved to Maine, where he underwent four years of biblical and theological training at Faith School of Theology. Upon returning to India in 2012, he began serving alongside our founding pastor, faithfully laboring in ministry and shepherding the church.

He was ordained as a pastor in 2014 and, following the passing of our founding pastor, became the Lead Pastor in 2020.

Pastor Joyson carries a vision to see thousands transformed by the Gospel and added to the church through our DJ Halli campus and to see many more churches planted for the glory of God. His passion is to see the Kingdom of God established through the faithful preaching of the Word and through the transforming, supernatural work of God in the lives of people.
