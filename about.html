Our Story

About Our Church

Shekinah Gospel Prayer Fellowship, Deverajeevanahalli Branch was born on September 27, 1992. We are a Bible-believing, Spirit-filled church dedicated to serving God and our community.

Our History & Calling

Shekinah Gospel Prayer Fellowship, Deverajeevanahalli Branch is a body of Christ with a divinely called ministry. Shekinah, D.J.Halli Branch was born on September 27, 1992 as per the leading of Lord Jesus Christ. It is a bible believing, Spirit filled Church with fruits and gifts operating.

We together as congregation, seek to serve God and others in our community, the commonwealth and around the world in His name. We welcome you to come and worship with us, experience Christian Fellowship and God's word from Pastor Joyson Dayalan at Shekinah Church, D J Halli Branch, Bangalore-45.

Our Vision

To propagate the gospel of Lord Jesus Christ to India and the world to know and to worship God as revealed in Lord Jesus Christ. To promote continued spiritual growth among the members.

Our Goal

To bring the Gospel Light to the city of Bangalore where darkness still prevails.

Our Community

Experience Christian Fellowship and God's word with Pastor Joyson Dayalan. We welcome you to worship with us.
